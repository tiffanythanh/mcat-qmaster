MCAT Passage Bank (offline)
===========================

Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). No internet needed.

What is inside
- Jack Westin free daily passages (972 dailies, 6,151 questions): AAMC-style passages for C/P, B/B,
  P/S and CARS, AAMC-style discrete sets, Fundamental passages and Fundamental discrete sets,
  each with Jack Westin's full solution and choice-by-choice explanations, difficulty, and
  the percent of students who got it right. Filter by source with the dropdown on the Dashboard.
- All Khan Academy MCAT practice passages (Units 2-5 of the Khan Academy MCAT course:
  Bio/Biochem, Chem/Phys, Psych/Soc, and CARS) plus the "Mini MCAT passages" and
  question sets from the Foundation 1-10 units.
- Every question with its answer choices, the correct answer, and Khan Academy's
  step-by-step explanation. Figures and tables are saved in the images/ folder.
- The sidebar organizes everything by UWorld subject and section (Behavioral Sciences,
  Biochemistry, Biology, General Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, Physics, CARS). Switch the
  dropdown to "Khan Academy order" to see the original unit/lesson layout.

Tracking progress
- Answer a question and click "Check answer" (or press Enter). Use keys A-D to pick a
  choice, arrow keys to move between questions, F to flag.
- Click the LETTER (A-D) to pick an answer; click the answer TEXT to cross it out (click again to undo).
- Select any passage or question text with the mouse to highlight it; click a highlight to remove it.
  Highlights are saved per passage.
- The passage list lives on the Dashboard (top-right button); the passage screen shows only the passage and questions.
- The sidebar shows, per section and per passage: number of questions, how many you have
  done, and your percent correct. The Dashboard shows overall and per-subject accuracy,
  flagged questions, and recent activity.
- Progress and notes are saved automatically in the browser (localStorage). They are tied
  to this folder's location, so if you move the folder use "Export progress" first and
  "Import" afterwards. Export regularly as a backup.
